The Home Journey

Eight chapters, one continuous walk through the basilica

The home route no longer reads as stacked sections. It's structured as numbered chapters, joined by transitional "thresholds" — gold ornaments, incense motes and a travelling light sweep that dissolve one hall's ground into the next.

Entrance

The Nave

An 8-layer volumetric hero — cathedral plate, colour grade, god-rays, fog, dust, an out-of-focus foreground edge, scrim and type — parallaxing on both scroll and pointer. Type is asymmetric and offset, never centred; only a whispered scripture reference breaks the silence. No visible CTA. Preceded once per session by "the Overture": black, then a shaft of light, then the great doors part and the nave is revealed.

Responsive

The hero on mobile

Every change in this build has been checked at 390px alongside desktop — motion, layout and type all degrade gracefully, and the Overture and Ambient layers respect prefers-reduced-motion.

The offset title, manuscript reference and near-invisible nav all hold at narrow widths. Ambient dust, god-rays and the scroll cue remain active; the foreground depth-of-field plane is dropped below 860px, where it would otherwise crowd the type.

Roadmap

What's next, and what's blocked

Blocked

Photographic consistency

The single biggest lever left. Several images (airport photo, laptop, mixed meeting shots) are inconsistent in tone and framing, and the cathedral interiors are AI-generated rather than authentic Roman/Vatican photography. This needs real source material from the client — it isn't something to solve in code.

Proposed

Gallery as exhibition

Move from a uniform grid to a featured-image-plus-supporting-images layout with varied heights, captions and a more cinematic lightbox transition.

Proposed

The Prophet as museum niche

Add a warm halo of light behind the portrait, a signature/seal mark, and a ministry milestone timeline in place of paragraph-only copy.

Proposed

Invitation as ceremony

Restructure the Invite page's opening as blessing, verse, prayer, then form — so the form reads as the culmination of a sequence rather than a UI block dropped after a banner.

Proposed

Extend the Overture

Currently about 6 seconds (doors, light, dust, title). A longer sequence is possible but risks impatience on repeat visits — worth an explicit decision before building further.
